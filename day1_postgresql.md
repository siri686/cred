\# Day 1 – PostgreSQL Setup and SQL Fundamentals



\## Objective

Set up PostgreSQL and practice creating tables, inserting data, applying constraints, and retrieving records.



\## Tables

\- `departments`: stores department IDs and unique department names.

\- `employees`: stores employee IDs, names, department IDs, and salaries.



\## Constraints Practiced

\- \*\*PRIMARY KEY:\*\* uniquely identifies each employee or department.

\- \*\*FOREIGN KEY:\*\* connects employees to departments.

\- \*\*NOT NULL:\*\* requires department names and employee names.

\- \*\*UNIQUE:\*\* prevents duplicate department names.

\- \*\*CHECK:\*\* prevents negative salary values.



\## SQL Concepts Practiced

\- CREATE TABLE

\- INSERT

\- SELECT

\- COUNT

\- INNER JOIN

\- LEFT JOIN

\- GROUP BY

\- AVG



\## Verification

\- Confirmed the department and employee row counts in pgAdmin.

\- Checked employee and department relationships using JOIN.

\- Calculated employee counts and average salaries by department.
